First-party content note
------------------------

The tone:// cue definitions under games/sample/audio and games/gauntlet/audio
are original parameter sets created for this repository. They contain no
sampled or third-party audio. The generated visual fixtures are documented in
games/sample/assets/README.md.

SYNC DRIVE product images and original synthesized WAV files are documented
with prompts, provenance, generation method, redistribution classification,
and SHA-256 values in ASSET-LICENSES.txt. They contain no third-party image or
audio source material. Projectile graphics are produced by the first-party
code renderer.
