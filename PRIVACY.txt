SYNC DRIVE Privacy Notice
=========================

Effective date: 2026-09-14

SYNC DRIVE does not transmit telemetry, analytics, account data, gameplay
records, crash reports, or personal information. Settings, local leaderboard
entries, profiles, and Replay files remain on the player's device under the
platform user-data directory.

Players who voluntarily submit the external playtest feedback form choose
what information to provide. Reproduction data should contain build ID,
platform, input/display/audio configuration, steps, expected/actual behavior,
and an optional Replay. Do not submit names, passwords, account tokens, or
unrelated files. Feedback is retained only as long as needed to evaluate the
test build or meet the repository owner's legal obligations.

Privacy contact: use the support channel listed in SUPPORT.txt.
