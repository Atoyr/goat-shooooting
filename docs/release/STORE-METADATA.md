# SYNC DRIVE store package

## Short description

Bank danger into power. SYNC DRIVE is a five-stage original bullet-hell shooter where grazing, point-blank attacks, cancels, and high-line shard collection feed a three-segment drive that trades safety for score and rank.

## Long description

Choose Vector's wide control, Lancer's focused speed, or Halo's orbiting crossfire across a 22–27 minute route. Novice teaches survival with auto-bomb, Arcade is the intended first clear, and Expert adds speed, density, health, and dynamic rank pressure. Activate one to three SYNC segments for stronger fire, score boost, and soft-bullet cancel; bank the resulting shards above the collection line before the chain decays. Practice every stage and all 15 boss checkpoints, save deterministic Replays, and compete across local mode/difficulty/ship leaderboard categories.

Features: five stages and five multi-phase bosses; three ships and three difficulties; signature campaign and Score Attack; stage/Boss Training; Replay viewer; local leaderboard; English/Japanese UI; remappable keyboard and controller input; reduced-motion/high-contrast accessibility preset; offline play with no telemetry.

## System requirements

Minimum: Windows 10 64-bit, dual-core 2.5 GHz CPU, 4 GB RAM, OpenGL 2.0-capable GPU with 1 GB VRAM, 500 MB storage, keyboard or XInput-compatible controller, 1280×720 display.

Recommended: Windows 11 64-bit, quad-core 3.0 GHz CPU, 8 GB RAM, dedicated OpenGL 3.3-capable GPU with 2 GB VRAM, SSD, 1920×1080 display, Xbox-compatible controller.

These specifications are targets pending the physical minimum/recommended hardware matrix; they are not yet verified claims.
